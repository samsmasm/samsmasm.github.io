Lineup is a card sort for any subject. Students drag cards onto a line between two opposite ends, tap Reveal to read a note on each card, and answer one reflection question. Your class screen shows where everyone put each card, so you can talk about the ones that split the class.

You make a deck of cards with any AI chatbot: Lineup writes the prompt for you. Students never sign in. Only teachers do.
