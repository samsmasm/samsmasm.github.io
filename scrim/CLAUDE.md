# Scrim, `/scrim`

Collaborative note/web canvas with pan/zoom, connectors, JPG/TXT export and password-protected rooms.

- `index.html`, `script.js`, `styles.css`. Firebase project `dowserboard` (shared with Dowser, Pulse-era tools).

Static, no build step; deploys on push to `main`. Follow the root `CLAUDE.md` (and `AESTHETIC.md` for anything new).
