Trust them, or take the points?

You and another player each pick cooperate or defect, at the same time, for 100 rounds. Defecting always pays more this round. So why do the best players cooperate?

3 Compete

Your teacher runs a tournament. Everyone plays everyone. Most points in total wins.

The rules in full
  • Each match is a set number of rounds, usually 100. Your teacher can change it.
  • Your strategy can see what happened earlier in the match, the round number and how many rounds are left.
  • It cannot see who it is playing, or anything from other matches.
  • You score against every strategy in the class, and maybe against a copy of yourself. Your total across all of them is your score. You do not need to beat anyone to win.
  • Nothing is random unless a strategy chooses to be. Every tournament can be replayed exactly from its seed.
