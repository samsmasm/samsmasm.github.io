Stay loyal, or sell them out?

You and another prisoner each pick cooperate or defect, at the same time, for 100 rounds. Defecting always means less jail this round. So why do the best players cooperate?

1 Design

Decide how you play: start nice or mean, hit back or forgive, hold a grudge or not.

2 Build

Click through the builder, or describe it to an AI and paste in what it gives you. Test it, then send it.

3 Compete

In your class, or in a public competition. Everyone plays everyone. Fewest years in jail wins.

The rules in full
  • Each match is a set number of rounds, usually 100. Your teacher can change it.
  • Your strategy can see what happened earlier in the match, the round number and how many rounds are left.
  • It cannot see who it is playing, or anything from other matches.
  • You play every other strategy, and maybe a copy of yourself. Your years in jail across all of those matches add up to your total. Fewest years wins. You do not need to beat anyone in a match to win overall.
  • Nothing is random unless a strategy chooses to be. Every tournament can be replayed exactly from its seed.
