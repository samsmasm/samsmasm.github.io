Before you start

This year, I (Sam) am doing some research on my use of AI. The key question is what types of resources are useful for you, and how you perceive them. I have two versions of this website so I can do A/B testing. You will be randomly allocated a different version of the website. Both are accurate. Occasionally you will be asked three quick questions. Go with your gut on your answers, though be honest. You are anonymous; no personal data is collected beyond your answers and which pages you visit.

If you do not want to be part of this, click 'Just give me the original website'; If you can help me help you, click 'Help Sam collect data to get better.'
