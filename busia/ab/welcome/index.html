Before you start

This year, I (Sam) am researching which kinds of resources are useful to you, and how you experience them. There are two versions of this guide. If you take part, you'll be randomly given one of them for the next two weeks. Both are accurate and cover what you need. Occasionally you'll be asked three quick questions about the page you're on. Go with your gut, but be honest: there are no right answers, and nothing you say affects your marks. It's anonymous: nothing is collected beyond your answers and which pages you visit. You can stop at any time with the "Stop the questions" link in the menu. After two weeks, everyone can choose either version.
