# NZ maths curriculum, Years 1 to 4 (for planning Sums)

Source: *The New Zealand Curriculum, Mathematics and Statistics Years 0–10*, November 2026 (in force 1 January 2027). Checked against the October 2025 year-by-year teaching sequences: Years 1–4 content is the same. Probability starts in Year 5, so it is not here.

**Years.** Year 1 = first year at school (turns 5 to 6). Year 2 = 6–7, Year 3 = 7–8, Year 4 = 8–9. So the 5-year-old (US kindergarten) is about Year 1, and the nearly-8-year-old (US grade 2, above level) is Year 3 going on Year 4.

**Marks.** ✓ = a Sums activity already covers it (name in brackets). ✋ = hands-on (real objects, moving around, class surveys), so it needs adapting to fit a screen. Everything else could be a new activity.

Each year lists **skills** (what she does). Facts she needs to know come after "knows:".

---

## Year 1 (first year at school)

Year 1 starts with a "first six months" stage; those skills are marked *(6m)*.

### Number
- Read and write numbers to 20 *(6m)*, then to 100. ✓ (Teen numbers, Place value)
- Count forwards and backwards from any number, within 10 then 20 *(6m)*, then within 100. ✓ (Number patterns, Year 1)
- Count forwards and backwards in 2s and 10s, 0–100. Knows: counting in 2s from 0 gives even numbers, from an odd number gives odd. ✓ (Number patterns, Number lines)
- Say the number before or after, to 10 *(6m)* then 20.
- Compare and order numbers to 20 *(6m)*, then to 100; ordinals 1st to 5th *(6m)*, then with suffixes (1st, 2nd, 3rd...) to 100.
- Put numbers on a fully labelled number line *(6m)*, then on a partly labelled one (17 on a line marked in 5s). ✓ partly (Number lines)
- Subitise (see without counting) 3–5 objects *(6m)*, then smaller groups inside a bigger set (3 and 5 in 8). ✓ (How many?)
- Count up to 10 objects one by one *(6m)*, then up to 20, also in pairs. ✓ (How many?)
- More, less or the same *(6m)*.
- Find totals to 20 by grouping in pairs, 5s or 10s. Knows: ten ones is one 10; 12 is six pairs or one 10 and two ones. ✓ (Teen numbers)
- Numbers in te reo Māori to 30.

### Operations
- Addition and subtraction facts to 5 *(6m)*, then to 10 (including 10 + 0). ✓ (Sums, Number bonds, Make 10)
- Doubles and halves to 10. ✓ (Doubles and halves, Year 1)
- Add and subtract one- and two-digit numbers within 20, including 0. ✓ (Sums, Add and take away Year 1)
- Join and separate groups to 20 (9 + 6, 7 + __ = 11). ✓ (Missing number, Number bonds)
- Add ten to a one-digit number (10 + 4). ✓ (Add and take away, Year 1)
- One-step + and − word problems with objects and pictures. ✓ (Story sums, Year 1, read aloud; no pictures)
- Multiply and divide by equal groups or sharing, answers within 20. ✓ (Times and divide Year 1, Story sums, Arrays)
- Knows: adding 0 changes nothing; order doesn't matter when adding but does when subtracting.

### Fractions and money
- Halves and quarters of sets, amounts and shapes, using equal parts. ✓ partly (Fractions)
- Find a half or quarter of a set by sharing; ½ of ½ is ¼.
- Know NZ coins and notes and their values. ✓ (Money, Year 1)

### Algebra (patterns and number sentences)
- Open number sentences with one-digit numbers (2 + 5 = 3 + __). ✓ partly (Missing number)
- True or false? (7 − 5 = 6 − 4)
- Copy, continue and make repeating patterns: two elements *(6m)* (cat, dog, cat, dog...), then three.
- Find the missing element (red, green, blue, red, __, blue).
- Ordinals to 5th for positions in a pattern.

### Measurement
- Compare two things by length, weight or capacity, directly *(6m)*, then using a third object. Words: longer, shorter, taller, heavier, lighter. ✋ (could be pictures)
- Days of the week in order, the day before and after *(6m)*; weekdays and weekend.
- Tell the time to the hour (o'clock) on analogue and digital clocks. ✓ (Clocks, Year 1)
- Choose a sensible unit for how long things take (seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years).
- Put events of the day in order (before, after, yesterday, tomorrow); parts of the day (morning, noon, evening, midnight).

### Geometry
- Name and sort 2D shapes by one attribute: triangle, circle, rectangle, square *(6m)*. Knows: a shape keeps its name whatever its size or colour.
- Name and sort 3D shapes in different positions: cube, cylinder, sphere.
- Make a shape from smaller shapes, and break one up.
- Position words: above, below, left, right, in front, behind, inside, outside, next to.
- Follow directions to a place or object ✋; describe positions with pictures or simple maps.

### Statistics
- Collect category data for a question (whose foot is longer?) ✋, and tally it.
- Make a picture graph.
- Read a picture graph: how many in each group, which has most or least.

## Year 2

### Number
- Read, write and show numbers to 120 with tens and ones (and hundreds to 120). ✓ (Place value)
- Compare and order numbers to 120.
- Place value of each digit in two-digit numbers and three-digit numbers to 120. ✓ (Place value)
- Roughly place numbers on a number line marked in tens (61 on a line marked 0, 10, 20...). ✓ partly (Number lines)
- Count a collection to 120 by making groups of ten.
- Round numbers to 120 to the nearest 10.
- Count in 2s, 5s and 10s forwards and backwards from any number to 120; count in 3s from multiples of 3. ✓ (Number patterns, Year 2)
- Odd and even numbers to 120 (last digit 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 = even).
- Numbers in te reo Māori to 100.

### Operations
- Addition and subtraction facts to 20 (17 + 3). ✓ (Sums, Number bonds)
- Derive facts from known ones (7 + 2 = 9 so 70 + 20 = 90).
- Add and subtract within 100 (32 + 20, 32 + 2). ✓ (Sums)
- Doubles and halves to 20. ✓ (Doubles and halves, Year 2)
- Add three one-digit numbers (7 + 3 + 6).
- Add 100 to a one-digit number.
- One-step + and − word problems to 100; two-step to 20. ✓ (Story sums, Year 2)
- Link skip counting to the 2, 5 and 10 times tables; learn those tables and their division facts. ✓ (Times bonds, Times grid)
- Multiply and divide with answers up to 100. ✓ (Sums, Arrays)
- Knows: × 1 and ÷ 1 change nothing; × 0 is 0; order doesn't matter for × but does for ÷; + and − undo each other, and so do × and ÷.

### Fractions and money
- Halves, thirds and quarters (½, ⅓, ⅔, ¼, ²⁄₄, ¾) of sets, amounts and shapes, in words and symbols. ✓ (Fractions)
- ²⁄₄ is the same as ½. ✓ (Fractions accepts equal fractions)
- Compare two fractions (halves are bigger than thirds, bigger than quarters).
- Find a half, third or quarter of a set by seeing groups, not sharing one by one.
- Find the whole from a part (if ½ is 5, the whole is 10).
- Order NZ coins and notes by value; group like coins and total them. ✓ partly (Money, Year 2: totals)
- Make an amount using only coins or only notes. ✓ partly (Money, Year 2: totals, not making)

### Algebra
- True or false comparisons to 120 (16 > 60?), using >, < and =.
- True or false and open number sentences with +, −, × and ÷ (18 + __ = 17 + 6, 6 ÷ __ = 2, 2 + 2 + 2 = 3 × 2?). ✓ partly (Missing number)
- Find the unit of repeat in a pattern and use it to predict what comes later (ACDC in ACDCACDC...).

### Measurement
- Measure length, weight or capacity with an informal unit used again and again (paper clips). ✋
- Compare and order several objects by those units.
- Estimate and measure in cm, g and mL with a marked tool (ruler, scale, jug). ✋ (a ruler on screen works)
- Perimeter of a polygon in metric units. ✓ (Area: Perimeter)
- Full, half, quarter and three-quarter turns, clockwise and anticlockwise.
- Months and seasons in order; the month before and after; ordinals for months.
- Describe how long familiar things take.
- Tell the time to the hour, half hour and quarter hour ("half past", "quarter past"). Knows: 60 minutes in an hour, 30 in half an hour. ✓ (Clocks, Year 2)

### Geometry
- Name and sort 2D and 3D shapes by attributes: oval, semicircle, pentagon, hexagon, cuboid, pyramid, cone. Words: edge, face, vertex.
- Flip, slide and turn 2D shapes to make a pattern or a shape.
- Give and follow directions with steps and half and quarter turns ✋ (could be a grid game).
- Read diagrams to say where things are relative to each other.

### Statistics
- Collect category data (favourite pets) ✋, sort it, decide if "other" is needed, tally it.
- Make picture graphs and dot plots.
- Describe them and answer most and least questions.

## Year 3

### Number
- Read, write and show numbers to 1,000 with base-ten blocks. ✓ (Place value: Blocks)
- Compare and order numbers to 1,000.
- Place value of each digit in three-digit numbers. ✓ (Place value)
- Count collections beyond 120 by grouping in 10s and 100s.
- Round to the nearest 10 or 100; estimate an answer before working it out.
- Count forwards and backwards in 2s, 3s, 4s, 5s and 8s from multiples (8, 16, 24, 32). ✓ (Number patterns, Year 3)
- Count in 10s and 100s from any number to 1,000. ✓ (Place value: 10 more / 10 less, Number lines)

### Operations
- Complements to 100 (34 + __ = 100).
- Add and subtract within 1,000 (329 + 3, 329 + 80, 329 − 200, 137 + 54), including column methods with regrouping. ✓ (Sums)
- Derive facts with place value (7 + 2 = 9 so 700 + 200 = 900).
- One-step + and − word problems to 1,000; two-step to 100. ✓ (Story sums, Year 3)
- Times tables and division facts for 2, 3, 4, 5, 8 and 10. ✓ (Times bonds, Times grid)
- Multiply and divide by sharing, grouping, repeated adding or known facts. ✓ (Arrays)
- Multiply a one- or two-digit number by a one-digit number (4 × 6, 2 × 23). ✓ (Sums)
- Divide by a one-digit number, no remainders (24 ÷ 3). ✓ (Sums ÷)
- Knows: dividing by zero is impossible.

### Fractions and money
- Read, write and show fractions of sets, amounts, shapes and on a number line, small denominators. ✓ (Fractions)
- Count in unit fractions up to 1 (⅕, ⅖, ⅗...).
- Compare unit fractions with denominators to 12 (bigger denominator = smaller piece: 1⁄12 < ⅙).
- Compare fractions with the same denominator (⅜ < ⅝).
- Spot equal fractions with pictures (³⁄₆ = ½; ²⁄₂ = ³⁄₃ = 1). ✓ (Fractions accepts them)
- Add and subtract fractions with the same denominator within one (⅛ + ²⁄₈ + ³⁄₈ = ⁶⁄₈).
- Unit fraction of a number by dividing (⅓ of 15 = 15 ÷ 3).
- Whole from a unit fraction (¼ of a set is 3, so the set is 12).
- Write money as dollars and cents without a decimal point ($2 and 50 cents). ✓ (Money, Year 3)
- Make amounts with $1 and $2 coins and $5, $10, $20, $50 and $100 notes. ✓ partly (Money: totals, not making)
- Give change by adding and subtracting. ✓ (Money: Change)

### Algebra
- True or false comparisons to 1,000 (313 < 330?).
- True or false and open number sentences with all four operations (217 − __ = 105, 12 ÷ 3 = 5 − 2?). ✓ partly (Missing number)
- Recognise, continue and make growing number patterns (a rule that adds or takes away each time). ✓ partly (Number patterns, Year 3: continue, not make)

### Measurement
- Estimate and measure in cm and m, g and kg, mL and L; compare and order by those units. ✋ (rulers and scales could be pictures)
- Perimeter of polygons. ✓ (Area: Perimeter)
- Area of rectangles by counting equal squares. ✓ (Area: Count squares)
- Full, half, quarter and three-quarter turns, clockwise and anticlockwise.
- How long events take, in seconds up to years.
- Tell the time to the nearest 5 minutes and nearest minute ("10 past", "quarter to", a.m. and p.m.). ✓ (Clocks, Years 3 and 4; not a.m. and p.m.)
- Compare units of time. Knows: 15 minutes in a quarter hour, 60 seconds in a minute, 24 hours in a day, 52 weeks and 365 days in a year (366 in a leap year, every 4 years), months about 4 weeks with different numbers of days, decade 10 years, century 100, millennium 1,000.

### Geometry
- Name, picture and sort regular polygons up to 10 sides (all sides and angles equal). Words: right angle, horizontal, vertical.
- Find lines of symmetry; complete a symmetrical pattern or picture.
- Step-by-step directions with half and quarter turns and distances; north, east, south, west.
- Use simple maps to find things relative to other things.

### Statistics
- Collect and sort category data ✋; collect counts (teeth lost, shoe sizes) ✋.
- Make dot plots and bar graphs.
- Describe them and answer questions, including ones that need sums (how many teeth did the class lose altogether?).

## Year 4

### Number
- Read, write, compare and order numbers to 10,000 with base-ten structure. Knows: each place is 10 times the one on its right; 0 holds a place.
- Round to the nearest 10, 100 or 1,000; round tenths to the nearest whole.
- Count forwards and backwards in 2s to 9s, 25s and 50s from multiples.
- Count in 10s, 100s and 1,000s from any number to 10,000.

### Operations
- Add and subtract up to four-digit numbers, mentally or in columns. ✓ (Sums, 1000–9999)
- All times tables and division facts, 2 to 10. ✓ (Times bonds, Times grid)
- Multiply and divide mentally with place value and known facts, including × 0, × 1 and ÷ 1.
- Multiply a two- or three-digit number by a one-digit number. ✓ (Sums)
- Divide up to a three-digit number by a one-digit number, no remainders (65 ÷ 5). ✓ (Sums ÷)
- Scale with × and ÷ (double or halve a recipe).

### Fractions, decimals and money
- Tenths as fractions and decimals (³⁄₁₀ = 0.3); compare and order them.
- Know ½ = 0.5 and other tenths as decimals.
- Divide one- and two-digit numbers by 10 to make tenths (7 ÷ 10 = 0.7); multiply tenths by 10.
- Compare and order fractions with the same top or same bottom number.
- Place fractions, improper fractions and mixed numbers on a number line (⁹⁄₈ = 1⅛).
- Spot equal fractions with pictures. ✓ (Fractions)
- Add and subtract fractions with the same denominator, past one whole (³⁄₈ + ³⁄₈ + ³⁄₈ = ⁹⁄₈ = 1⅛).
- Add and subtract decimals to one place (1.3 + 0.2 = 1.5).
- Unit fraction of a number (⅓ of 300); the whole from a unit fraction (¼ of a set is 7, so the set is 28).
- Total cost of several items in whole dollars, or many of one item, and the change.
- Make an amount in different ways ($5 and 80 cents).

### Algebra
- True or false and open number sentences with + and − (8205 − 4721 = 3484?; 4200 − __ = 4001). ✓ partly (Missing number, Sums 1000–9999)
- The same with × and ÷ (11 × 7 = 78?; __ ÷ 10 = 12). ✓ partly (Missing number)
- Knows: doing the same thing to both sides keeps it balanced.
- Growing patterns that add, take away or multiply by the same number each time (5, 7, 9, 11...; 3, 6, 12, 24...): continue, make and describe them.

### Measurement
- Make estimation benchmarks from familiar things (body parts, the walk to school). ✋
- Measure in mixed units with the right tool (1 m and 23 cm, 2 L and 500 mL). ✋ partly
- Temperature in degrees Celsius (a thermometer picture works).
- Perimeter in mm, cm and m. ✓ (Area: Perimeter)
- Area of irregular shapes from squares and half squares.
- Area of rectangles by multiplying the sides. ✓ (Area: Area, Missing side)
- Volume of cuboids by filling with cubes.
- Estimate angles against 90°, 180° and 360°. Knows: a full turn is 360°, a half turn 180°, a quarter turn 90°; rectangles have four right angles.
- Tell the time to the nearest minute, analogue and digital. ✓ (Clocks, Year 4)
- Durations in mixed units (1 h 42 min, 3 min 21 s).
- Change between time units (3 weeks = 21 days, 90 seconds = 1½ minutes, 48 hours = 2 days).

### Geometry
- Classify regular and irregular polygons up to 12 sides by edges, vertices and angles; quadrilaterals (square, rectangle, rhombus, parallelogram, trapezium, kite); parallel lines.
- Count lines of symmetry in 2D shapes (a circle has infinitely many).
- Match 3D shapes to 2D drawings and views from different sides.
- One-step reflections, translations and rotations of 2D shapes.
- Grid references (A1, B2) to find squares and plot positions on a map.

### Statistics
- Collect numerical data, rounding if needed (skips in 30 seconds) ✋.
- Make dot plots and bar graphs with a title, labels, units and a scale from 0.
- Read dot plots: the frequency of a value, individual values, the shape, the middle, the spread (lowest to highest). Tell "the value" from "how many had that value" apart (the most pets anyone has vs the most common number of pets).
