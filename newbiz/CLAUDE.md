# Newbiz, `/newbiz`

Random-word business case generator: spin unrelated words on a 3D drum and build a business case from what lands.

- `index.html`, `words.js` (word buckets and constraints).
- Chrome-free by design: no site header or nav. Keep it that way.

Static, no build step; deploys on push to `main`. Follow the root `CLAUDE.md` (and `AESTHETIC.md` for anything new).
