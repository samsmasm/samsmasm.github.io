Newbiz

Spin two or three unrelated words and build a business case out of whatever lands. Every reel draws from a different category, so you never get two words that are basically the same idea. Add a constraint if you want a problem rather than just a product.

Words
Constraint
What to do with it. Take the words at face value and force a connection. Then work through:
  1. Who is the customer, specifically? Not "everyone".
  2. What problem does this solve that something else does not?
  3. What does one unit cost you, and what would someone pay?
  4. What is the single biggest reason this fails?
