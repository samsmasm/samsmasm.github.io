Mapping tools

For runs, rides and routes.
unisam.nz

Your files stay in your browser. The map pictures come from CARTO, using OpenStreetMap data, and they only see which bit of the map you are looking at.

Tools

GPS Fixer
Redraw the bit of a run or ride where your GPS dropped out, then upload the fixed GPX to Strava.
Mapic
Draw a picture on the map, or trace an image, and turn it into a route along real roads for GPS art.

Elsewhere on unisam.nz

Longcut
Find a walking or cycling route that favours quiet back streets and paths over main roads.
