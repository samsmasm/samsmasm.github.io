1Find your area

Switch to Pan (top right of the map) to move and zoom the map to where the route should go. Pinch or scroll to zoom in either mode.

2Image to trace (optional)

Choose an image or drop it here
See-through

In Pan mode: drag the image to move it, the white corner dot to resize it, and the blue dot to rotate it.

3Draw

  • In Draw mode, draw with a mouse, finger or pen. Each new stroke joins on to the end of the line (or the start, if you begin near it).
  • In Pan mode, drag a pink dot to move it, or click it to remove it.
  • Undo and redo: top right of the map, or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y.
–

4Follow the roads

Coming next: turn the drawing into a route along real roads and paths, slide and stretch it to find a good fit, and download it as a GPX file.

Privacy: your image and drawing stay in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. Map tiles (OpenStreetMap data, served by CARTO) reveal roughly which area you are looking at, as any web map does.
