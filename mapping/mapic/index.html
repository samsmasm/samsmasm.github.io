1Find your area

Switch to Pan (top right of the map) to move and zoom the map to where the route should go. Pinch or scroll to zoom in either mode.

2Image to trace (optional)

Choose an image or drop it here
See-through

In Pan mode: drag the image to move it, the white corner dot to resize it, and the blue dot to rotate it.

3Draw

  • In Draw mode, draw with a mouse, finger or pen. You can use several strokes: a stroke that starts or ends near the end of another joins on to it.
  • In Pan mode, drag a pink dot to move it, or click it to remove it.
  • In Adjust mode, move, resize, stretch or rotate the whole drawing. The route follows as you drag.
  • Undo and redo: top right of the map, or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y.
–

4Follow the roads

Finds the route along roads and paths that stays closest to your drawing. After the first snap it updates by itself whenever you change the drawing.

Route for
  • Not the road you wanted? In Pan mode, drag the green route onto another road (on a touchscreen, tap the route first, then drag the dot). Click a green dot to remove it.

5Download

A GPX track with no times, for route planners and watches (Strava, Garmin, Komoot and so on).

Privacy: your image and drawing stay in this browser. Map tiles (OpenStreetMap data, served by CARTO) reveal roughly which area you are looking at, as any web map does. When you snap to roads, map data for the area around your drawing is downloaded from OpenFreeMap, which sees which map tiles are fetched; the drawing itself is not sent, and the route is worked out in this browser.
