1/1/96
24 Gordon St
Dargaville

Dear Dell.
I copied the write up out of 'Early Northern Wairoa' because I did not know when I could get down the Street. I think there is more chance of you finding your Fathers relations or history than any of the Dawes's. Most of Dads would be over 100 that he kept contact with. I know Ada Cundill is dead but not to sure about Annie Dawes. Some of Grandma Dawes's might still exist thats the Peet's. The Maggie Hunt is Ada Cundills sister & her daughter might still be living. According to Photo she would be about 76 now. I think her husband was lost in the Submarine "The Thetis" I think that was the name of the "Sub" during last war. If it wasn't the Daughters husband that was lost it was one of Maggies Sons. We used to send food parcels to Dads relations during last war. But have no records. I know Dad did. But the Gissings never gave us any of Dads letters or things back. I got these out of a little tin trunk Dad left with me when he & Dorothea got married.

Your Father was George Eric Paton born Oct 2nd 1901. He was a grand son of John Paton who came out in The 'Fifeshire' in 1842. George Paton & Alice Russel were his parents & Ernie Paton pinched Alice from George. But some of Georges relations told me they loved George & Ernie both. but George threw Ernie & Alice together. (I dont know or care) but Eric always blamed Ernie. So long now sorry cant give any more details. Love Mum.
