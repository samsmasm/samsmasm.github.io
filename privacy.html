Privacy

unisam.nz · last updated 10 September 2026

unisam.nz is a collection of free teaching and revision tools, built by a teacher for students. Most of it is plain static web pages that ask nothing of you at all. This page explains the exceptions.

The short version

  • You can use nearly everything here without signing in or giving any information.
  • Nothing on this site is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build a profile of you.
  • Where a tool does sign you in, it says so, and it stores only what it needs to do its job.

Analytics

Pages on this site count visits using GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service. It records anonymous aggregate page views. It sets no cookies, does not track you between sites, and does not identify individual visitors.

Fonts and other external requests

Some pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, and some load mapping or charting libraries from public CDNs. When your browser fetches those files, the provider receives the request as it would for any website.

Tools that sign you in with Google

ShiftWork, the economics graph maker, can optionally sign you in with Google so you can save diagrams. Signing in is not required, and the tool works fully without it using its copy-and-paste save code instead.

If you do sign in, it asks Google for two things:

  • Your email address and basic profile, used only to show which account is signed in. It is displayed on your own screen and sent nowhere else.
  • Permission to manage files it creates in your Drive (the drive.file scope). This is the narrowest Drive permission Google offers. It allows the tool to open and save only the diagram files it created itself. It cannot see, read, or search any other file in your Google Drive.

Your diagrams are saved into your own Google Drive, in a folder called ShiftWork Diagrams, as ordinary .json files. They belong to your Google account and use your own Drive storage. They are never copied to this site, which has no server and no database, so we have no way to view, collect or recover them. That includes your teacher. To hand a diagram in, export it as a JPG or share the file yourself.

Deleting a diagram from inside ShiftWork moves it to your Drive bin, so you can restore it yourself if you delete one by mistake.

You can disconnect the tool from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already saved stay in your Drive, and you can delete them there like any other file.

Limited use of Google user data

This site's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, never transferred to third parties except as required to provide the feature you asked for, never used for advertising, and never read by a human.

Classroom tools that save your work

A few of the classroom tools here, such as the revision and planning apps, save what you enter so it is still there when you come back. These use Google's Firebase service for storage. What they keep is limited to the content you put into the tool itself, such as your answers, progress or notes, along with a sign-in identifier where the tool requires an account.

This information is used only to make the tool work for you. It is not sold or shared, and it is not used for anything beyond the tool it belongs to. If you would like your data in one of these tools deleted, email the address below and it will be removed.

Children and schools

These tools are used by school students. No tool on this site asks for personal information beyond what is described above, and nothing here is used for advertising or profiling.
