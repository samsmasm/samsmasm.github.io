Classroom

Tools

Small, focused tools for running a lesson: live questioning and quizzes, ways to read the room, structured thinking, and a few private utilities. Most need nothing more than a browser.

Griz
Generate competitive class quizzes from your own content.
UniQuiz
Run live quiz sessions. Students join with a PIN and answer on their device.
Scrim
Pose a question, get student answers projected on your screen from their devices.
Spectra
Post statements, students rate on a scale, see the full spectrum of class opinion.
Pulse
Students plot themselves on your variables and see the class spread in real time.
Flare
Real-time help signals from students. See at a glance when your class needs support.
Argument Mapper
Build structured argument maps: claims, reasons, objections, rebuttals, evidence.
Dowser
Freeform canvas of moveable post-its, lines, and arrows for brainstorming.
Whiteboard
A basic, get-out-of-the-way whiteboard.
PeeDeeEffer
Merge, split, and extract text from PDFs privately, entirely on your own device.
PDF Annotator
Annotate a PDF and download your marked-up copy.
Cultivar
Vocabulary flashcards with spaced repetition for language learners.
