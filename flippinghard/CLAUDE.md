# Flipping Hard Work, `/flippinghard`

Live coin-flip betting game: teacher runs flips, students join a session and bet their balance.

- `index.html` (join), `student.html`, `teacher.html`. Firebase project `dowserboard` (shared with Dowser, Pulse-era tools).

Static, no build step; deploys on push to `main`. Follow the root `CLAUDE.md` (and `AESTHETIC.md` for anything new).
