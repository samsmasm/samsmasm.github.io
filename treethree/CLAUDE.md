# TREE(3) Tree Builder, `/treethree`

Build a sequence of coloured trees under TREE(3)-style rules by drag and drop.

- `index.html`, `script.js`, `styles.css`. Later iterations in `treethree-v2.1/` and `treethree-v2.2/`; check which one is linked before editing.

Static, no build step; deploys on push to `main`. Follow the root `CLAUDE.md` (and `AESTHETIC.md` for anything new).
