How the verdict works

Each signal is green, amber or red against fixed thresholds (open any card to see them). Red counts 1, amber counts ½. Under 1.5 is Not yet, under 3 is Wobbling, under 4.5 is Hissing, 4.5 or more is Popped. The thresholds are judgement calls, not science. Where would you put them?

About these numbers

AI index: equal-weighted index of the 21 US-listed stocks above (Asia excluded), rebased to 100. A stock joins on its listing day. From peak: below highest close since 2020. vs 200-day: above or below the 200-day average price. Death cross: 50-day average below the 200-day. Volatility: last month's daily swings, annualised. vs S&P: 3-month return minus the S&P 500's. Asian stocks are in local currency.

Prices: Yahoo Finance (adjusted closes) · Credit spread and rates: FRED · Capex, Nvidia, GPU prices: entered by hand after each earnings season. Not investment advice. Built by Sam G. Found a problem? Tell Sam.
