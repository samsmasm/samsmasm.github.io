How scoring works
  • Group matches: 1 pt for the right result (win / draw / win).
  • Group winners: 2 pts each.
  • Your chosen double-points group doubles its group-stage points (matches + winner).
  • Knockout progression: Round of 32 1, Round of 16 1, Quarterfinals 2, Semifinals 3, Final 4 per correct team.
  • 3rd-place playoff winner 5, Champion 10.
  • Final score is a tiebreaker (★), then alphabetical.
