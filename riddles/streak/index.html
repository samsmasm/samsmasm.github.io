The riddle

HHHHH?

You flip a fair coin. It lands heads five times in a row.

Your friend says tails is due.

What is the chance the next flip is heads?

Your guess

The chance of heads on the sixth flip.

Truly random. New flips every time you open this page, so the counts come out near even, not exactly.

What do you see?

After five heads, how did the sixth flip land?

The answer

50%
    Show the calculation
    Five heads in a row: 1/2 x 1/2 x 1/2 x 1/2 x 1/2 = 1/32 Out of 10,000 people: 10,000 / 32 = about 312 A class of 30, each flipping five coins: nobody gets five heads: 31/32 x 31/32 x ... (30 times) = about 39% at least one does: 100% - 39% = about 61% Five heads, then heads: 1/32 x 1/2 = 1/64 Five heads, then tails: 1/32 x 1/2 = 1/64 The two are equal. So after five heads, the next flip is still 50:50.
