The riddle

Sleeping Beauty falls asleep. We flip a coin. She does not see it.

HHeads: we wake her once TTails: we wake her twice

After each waking she falls asleep again and forgets she was woken. So every waking feels like the first.

She knows all these rules. She just doesn't know how the coin landed.

Each time we wake her, we ask: "What is the chance the coin landed heads?" What should she say?

Your guess

What should she say when she wakes? The chance of heads, as a percent.

The answer

Nobody agrees

Both counts are true. The argument is about which count answers her question.

Halfers say
1/2

Count coin flips. She knew it was 50:50 before she slept, and waking up tells her nothing new: she was always going to wake up.

Thirders say
1/3

Count wakings. Only 1 waking in 3 is a heads waking. If she bet "tails" every time she woke, she would win twice as often.
