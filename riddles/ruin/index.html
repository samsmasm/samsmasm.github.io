The riddle

You have $10. Each round you bet $1 on a coin flip against a casino. Heads, you win $1. Tails, you lose $1.

The game is fair: neither side has an edge. But the casino has endless money.

You keep playing, round after round, and only stop if you run out of money.

If you play long enough, what is the chance you go broke?

Your guess

The chance you end up with $0, if you keep playing.

The answer

100%

Play for long enough and you are certain to go broke, even in a fair game.

    Show the calculation
    In a fair game, the chance you go broke before the other side is: the other side's money --------------------------------- your money + the other side's money Against a friend with $10: 10 / (10 + 10) = 50% Against a casino with $1,000: 1,000 / (10 + 1,000) = 99.0% Against a casino with $1 million: 1,000,000 / (10 + 1,000,000) = 99.999% Against endless money: as close to 100% as you like How fast? With $10, the chance you are broke by: round 100: about 32% round 1,000: about 75% round 20,000: about 94%
