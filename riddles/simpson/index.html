The riddle

Doctors tested two treatments for kidney stones, A and B, on 700 real patients.

For patients with small stones, A worked more often.

For patients with large stones, A worked more often.

Across all 700 patients, which treatment worked more often?

Your guess

Which treatment worked more often overall?

Watch

What do you see?

Look at the bars after the groups merged.

The answer

B worked for 83% of its patients. A worked for 78%.

Small stones93%81 of 8787%234 of 270
Large stones73%192 of 26369%55 of 80
All patients78%273 of 35083%289 of 350

    Data: Charig and others, British Medical Journal, 1986. A was open surgery; B was a keyhole method.
