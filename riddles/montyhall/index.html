The riddle

A car is behind one door. Goats are behind the other two.

You pick a door. The host knows where the car is. He opens one of the other doors and shows you a goat.

Then he asks: do you want to switch to the last closed door?

Should you stay or switch?

Your guess

Which wins the car more often?

Play

Pick a door. After the host opens a goat door, click a door to stay or switch. Every game goes in the row for what you chose.

Stayed
Switched
Won the car
Got a goat
Not played yet

Truly random. The car is hidden at random every game, and differently each time you play. So the rows come out near 1 in 3 and 2 in 3, not exactly.

What do you see?

Look at the filled dots: the cars won. Which row has more?

The answer

Switch. Switching wins 2 games in 3.
