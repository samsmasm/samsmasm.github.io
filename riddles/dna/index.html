The riddle

A crime happens in a city of 10 million people.

Police search a DNA database of the whole city and find one match. Nothing else links him to the crime.

A random person matches by chance only 1 time in a million. In court, the prosecutor says:

"There is only a one in a million chance he is innocent."

How likely is it that he is innocent?
