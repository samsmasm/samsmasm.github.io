The riddle

People walk into an empty room, one at a time.

Each person's birthday is random. Ignore 29 February.

How many people do you need for a 50% chance that two of them share a birthday?

The answer

23 people
    Show the calculation
    Work out the chance of NO match, then flip it. Person 2 must miss person 1: 364/365 Person 3 must miss both: 363/365 ... Person 23 must miss all 22: 343/365 No match = 364/365 x 363/365 x ... x 343/365 = 0.493, or 49.3% Match = 100% - 49.3% = 50.7%, just over half Pairs among 23 people = 23 x 22 / 2 = 253
