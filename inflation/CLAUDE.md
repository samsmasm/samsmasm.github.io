# Who's Feeling It?, `/inflation`

NZ inflation by household group: headline CPI vs household living-cost price indexes, by demographic, income and housing.

- `index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`, `data/`. Chart.js. Data provenance in `data-sources.md`.

Static, no build step; deploys on push to `main`. Follow the root `CLAUDE.md` (and `AESTHETIC.md` for anything new).
