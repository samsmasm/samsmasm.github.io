Chinese imports plunge 30% to 40% after EU tax on small parcels

euronews.com

Since 1 July, the EU has levied a €3 customs duty on parcels worth under €150 and the measure is already biting as imports, especially from China, have slumped by 30% to 40%.

  • A €3 tax caused Chinese imports to drop by 30-40 percent. Why would such a small fee have such a huge effect, and what does this reveal about how price-sensitive online shoppers are?
  • The EU says this tax protects European businesses, but it also means Europeans will pay more for cheap goods online. Is it fair to make ordinary consumers pay higher prices to protect local industries, or should countries compete on quality and innovation instead?

euronews.com

Nvidia's $96.2bn quarter and bullish 2028 guidance eased some fears about an AI bubble, but its reliance on a handful of big customers, and a cautious re-entry into China, suggest the picture is more complicated.

  • Nvidia makes most of its money from just a few mega-customers (like OpenAI and big tech firms). Is this a strength because those customers are so committed, or a major risk if even one of them stops buying? How would you explain this to someone choosing whether to invest in Nvidia?

Korea tests Arctic route with first trial voyage in decade

koreaherald.com

South Korea has launched its first government-backed container ship voyage through the Arctic in a decade, testing whether a route 35 percent shorter than the Suez Canal passage can become commercially viable despite high operating costs, limited cargo and geopolitical risks.

  • The Arctic route saves 35 percent on distance but costs more to operate and carries geopolitical risks. Why would any shipping company use it unless costs come down significantly, and what would need to change to make it truly competitive?
  • Climate change is melting Arctic ice and opening new shipping routes, but it's also causing severe environmental damage. Should countries and companies exploit these new routes for profit, or should they be restricted to protect the Arctic environment? How do you balance economic opportunity with environmental responsibility?
