Concept check

Short question sets for a class: multiple choice that marks itself, and short written answers your teacher reads. Sign in with Google to start.

Students: sign in, then join your class with the code your teacher gives you.

How it works

Teachers Make a class, write questions or paste a CSV, then open a set to the class.
Students Join with a code. The current question is waiting on your class page.
Afterwards Scores for multiple choice, and your own words back for the written ones.
