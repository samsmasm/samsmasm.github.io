# Cat Jump 2!, `/catjump`

Kids' runner game: tap to jump, double tap for a big jump, collect fish, optional play-time limit.

- `index.html`, `script.js`, `styles.css`. Best score, fish and mute in localStorage (`catjump2-*`).
- Typurr (`typurr/`) branched from this. Old version kept at `catjumpold/`.

Static, no build step; deploys on push to `main`. Follow the root `CLAUDE.md` (and `AESTHETIC.md` for anything new).
