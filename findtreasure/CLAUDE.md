# Find the Treasure, `/findtreasure`

Hidden-treasure guessing game inside a shape (triangles, squares, countries), with adjustable win radius and click count.

- `index.html` plus `countries.geojson` (from the deprecated datahub geo-boundaries dataset; its `README.md` is the dataset's, not ours).

Static, no build step; deploys on push to `main`. Follow the root `CLAUDE.md` (and `AESTHETIC.md` for anything new).
