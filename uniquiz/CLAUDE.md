# UniQuiz, `/uniquiz`

Kahoot-style live quiz: teacher creates a quiz (CSV import), students join with a PIN.

- `index.html` (join), `create.html`, `play.html`, `js/` (auth, firebase, session, teacher, play, create), `css/`, music mp3s.
- Firebase project `dowserboard` (shared with Dowser, Pulse-era tools).

Static, no build step; deploys on push to `main`. Follow the root `CLAUDE.md` (and `AESTHETIC.md` for anything new).
