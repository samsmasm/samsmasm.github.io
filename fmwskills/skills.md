# FMW Skills Analysis (Grade 9 Foundations of the Modern World)

Purpose: map the **skills** (not knowledge/content) students build across the year, unit by unit, in plain teacher language. Built skill-first, not standards-first — the rubric criteria are cross-referenced where they genuinely fit, but skills that don't map cleanly are just left that way (noted as "loosely," "not on the rubric," etc.) rather than forced into a box.

Each skill is tagged **Introduced** (first real appearance) or **Reinforced** (building on something from an earlier unit) so we can later analyse sequencing across the year: where skills are scaffolded well, where they reappear too late or too rarely, where something is taught once and never revisited.

Source rubric criteria referenced below (from the house rubric bank used across most units): *Questions and Sources, Time Continuity and Change, Government/Geography/Society and Technology (swappable lens), Using Evidence, Communicating Conclusions.*
