# Conclusion

The most common failure in IA conclusions is a summary with no verdict. Answer the question, and do not dodge it.

> **Verdict first.** If your RQ is "Should Nike shift its manufacturing to sustainable suppliers?", say yes or no. "The evidence suggests Nike faces significant pressure to change its supply chain" is not an answer. "Nike should accelerate its transition to sustainable suppliers, primarily because..." is.

## What the conclusion needs to do

**1. Answer the research question directly**
State your judgment in the opening sentence. Hedging everything equally is a summary, not a conclusion.

**2. Summarise the key evidence**
Name the two or three findings that drove your verdict, with no new tools or data.

**3. Return to the key concept**
Say what this case reveals about how your concept works in real business decisions.

**4. Qualify your answer if you need to**
If your verdict depends on a condition, say so, based on points you have already made in your analysis. Do not raise new limitations here.

> **300 words is tight.** Cut any sentence that does not serve the verdict, the evidence or the concept.
