# What is the IB BM IA?

One research project, up to 1,800 words. It isn't an exam, it's independent research on a real business. You pick a business, ask a genuine question about it, gather evidence, apply business tools and theories, and reach a reasoned conclusion. Work through this guide in order.

## The basics

**1,800** words maximum
**3-5** supporting documents
**25** marks available
**30%** of your final grade (SL)
**20%** of your final grade (HL)

The limit is strict and moderators won't read beyond it. Appendices (supporting documents), diagrams, data tables, citations and references don't count. Definitions and quotes in the body do.

## The marking criteria

| Criterion | What it tests | Marks |
| --------- | ------------- | ----- |
| **A** | Use of a key concept throughout the research | 5 |
| **B** | Supporting documents: quality, range, and relevance | 4 |
| **C** | Use of business management tools and theories | 4 |
| **D** | Quality of analysis and evaluation | 5 |
| **E** | Conclusions linked to the research question | 3 |
| **F** | Structure and organisation | 2 |
| **G** | Presentation: title page, contents page, headings, page numbers | 2 |
| **Total** | | **25** |

A and D together are almost half the marks, and both reward deep, sustained thinking, not just knowledge.

## The four key concepts

Build your whole project around one: [Change](concept/#change), [Creativity](concept/#creativity), [Ethics](concept/#ethics) or [Sustainability](concept/#sustainability). It isn't a label for the introduction. It shapes your question, your tools, your analysis and your conclusion. More in [Key Concept](concept/).

## The big picture

Three sections: introduction (about 400 words), analysis and discussion (about 1,100), conclusion (about 300), supported by 3-5 appendix documents.

Everything connects back to your research question, and it shapes every choice that follows, so choosing a good one matters most (see [Find Your RQ](rq/)).

**Start here.** Don't rush into writing before the thinking is done.
