I

Sketching Ideas

This stage breaks the tyranny of the blank page. It is free writing: get as many ideas down as you can without criticising yourself. You are not writing your IA yet; you are finding out what you think.

Sketch your thinking

Write a sentence or two, fast, for each of these starters:

  • The key issue this company is facing is...
  • What seems certain is...
  • What is still uncertain or up in the air is...
  • At this stage, my hunch is that the company should...

Or set a timer for a five-minute free write. If you are stuck, open with: "The answer is... because... actually the answer might be... because..."
